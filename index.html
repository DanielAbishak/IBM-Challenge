Keep Going

These are our proposed improvements to the existing IBM SkillsBuild platform, not the app as it works today. SkillsBuild is currently a self-paced website with no offline access, no way to save your place in a long course, and only a scripted chatbot for help. This prototype shows three additions: download for offline, time-based sessions you can stop and resume, and an AI learning companion inside the lesson. Tap through the phone — everything responds.

Features

1

Offline access

Switch on airplane mode, then open Module 3 from the course screen. It plays. Module 4 was never downloaded, so it waits.

2

Time-based sessions, stop & resume

Pick how long you have on the Home screen, then tap Continue. The app counts toward that goal, tells you when you reach it, and offers to stop — keeping your exact second, with no streak to break.

3

AI learning companion

Ask from inside the lesson, in your own words. It answers from Module 3 only, and offers the questions learners rarely type but act on.
